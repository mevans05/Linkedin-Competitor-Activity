## Who we are
Zilker Trail is a boutique data, analytics, and digital transformation
consultancy. Relative to this competitor set, we sit between two poles:
large-scale generalist consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC,
McKinsey) and specialized digital/analytics shops (Apply Digital, Adswerve,
Insight Enterprises, Slalom).

## How we differentiate (draft — confirm or correct)
- **Speed and senior attention over scale**: smaller, senior-led teams vs. the
  large-firm staffing pyramid.
- **Practical, implementation-first** over slide-deck strategy — we ship
  working data/analytics infrastructure, not just recommendations.
- **Platform depth** (e.g., GA4 / Google Marketing Platform, cloud data
  stacks) rather than broad generalist coverage.
- **Client-side pragmatism**: point solutions to real measurement and data
  problems over multi-year transformation programs.

## Voice
- Direct, technically credible, allergic to consulting jargon.
- Willing to take a specific point of view rather than hedge.
- Speaks from practitioner experience, not thought-leadership generalities.

## Topics we care about having a POV on
- Marketing/data measurement, analytics platforms, GA4/GMP, MarTech stack
  decisions
- AI adoption in marketing and data functions (practical, not hype)
- Data governance, privacy-driven measurement changes
- Client-side vs. agency-side data ownership

## Topics/POVs we should be skeptical of or push back on
- Big-consultancy "transformation" framing that overpromises on timeline/ROI
- Thought leadership that's directionally vague (trend-spotting without a
  practical recommendation)
